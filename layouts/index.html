🧭 About the Author

Ashiq Alibhai is a Muslim author and lifelong reader of fantasy and science fiction. His work explores the meeting point between faith, Islamic themes, and speculative worlds. His books are available worldwide in digital and physical formats through bookstores and libraries. He lives near Toronto with his family and their cat Marshmallow.
