What if Star Wars' species were all anthropomorphic walking talking animals instead?

This idea gripped me and refused to let go: an epic space saga where snakes fight cats, dogs with swords defend their kingdom, and intragalactic space combat is the norm. Inspired by Ninjago's Serpentine and Puss in Boots (among others), I decided to embark on an epic literary adventure.

Unfortunately, my lack of specific ideas came back to bite me. I wrote pages upon pages of notes about species' unique culture, weaponry, naming, down to minute details. But it all formed a rather large and overwhelming list of things to conform my writing to. How stressful!

So instead, after the initial inspiration died, I decided in late 2025 to give the idea another try. This time, I removed everything but the absolute minimum: space-faring anthropomorphic animals (hence the series name: *Starborn Beasts*) and instead focused on writing small stories to introduce readers to the universe, the species, and the exact interesting events that unfold.

The resulting work formed into **The Coming Swarm,** the first book, and a stepping-stone of sorts, into this new universe. It chronicles the tale of eight different species, and focuses around the coming of a ninth, extremely dangerous alien race, the Chyrrix.  Five of the stories directly relate to each other.

If the idea of snakes and birds waging interplanetary war sounds interesting, give the book a try. You can [read more about the book here](/books/2025/the-coming-swarm/). It's currently available in e-book format, with print format coming soon, inshaAllah.
