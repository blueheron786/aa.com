Due to the ongoing genocide of Palestinians and occupation of Palestine, we no longer list links to purchase our books from Indigo.

[According to Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gerry_Schwartz), the founders, Gerry Schwartz and his wife Heather Reisman, co-found the [Heseg Foundation for Lone Soldiers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HESEG_Foundation):

> The organization provides millions of dollars of support in the form of scholarships, a living wage and career development opportunities as a reward for military service with the Israeli Defense Forces (IDF) every year to non-Israeli Jews who volunteered to join the Israeli military and to some Israeli soldiers as well.

Don't buy your books from companies whose owners donate to support apartheid and genocide of indigenous peoples.

You can read more at the [CJPME Factsheet about Indigo](https://www.cjpme.org/fs_238).
