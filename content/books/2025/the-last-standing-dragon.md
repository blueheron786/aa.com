When Bilal's mother vanishes after a mysterious mission, the young golden dragon refuses to sit and wait. With his fiery sister, grumpy brother, and a band of loyal friends, he takes flight across seas and storms in search of her. But the trail leads to a cursed island-where the forest whispers, vines breathe, and shadows remember.

Together they must unravel an ancient secret, outwit an ogre, and trust an unlikely wyvern ally whose painful past ties him to their enemy. And looming over it all is the Empress, whose dark magic feeds on life itself.

The Last Standing Dragon is a heart-pounding middle grade fantasy about family, friendship, and the courage to face a shadow older than dragons themselves.
